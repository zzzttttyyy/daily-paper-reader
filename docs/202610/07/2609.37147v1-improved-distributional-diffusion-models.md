## 摘要
分布扩散模型（DDMs）用通过评分规则目标训练的分布去噪器取代标准的均值预测去噪器，学习对 $p(x_1 \mid x_t)$ 的随机近似，而不是其条件均值。然而，将 DDM 扩展到现代图像生成场景面临两个障碍：(i) 多粒子训练带来的开销随粒子数量增长；(ii) DDM 使用全局固定的评分规则超参数，迫使在所有采样预算上采用单一权衡。我们通过将粒子扩展推迟到 Transformer 后层来缓解这些限制，并通过引入受 Biroli 等 (2024) 的动力学区域启发的时间相关评分规则调度来缓解超参数权衡。结合基于 DiT 的潜空间设置，这些改动使 DDM 训练在类别条件 ImageNet-$256^2$ 上变得实用，使用 DiT-XL/2 在 4 步达到 4.48 FID、在 50 步达到 2.38，且来自一个单阶段从零训练的单一模型，无需教师模型、自蒸馏或 JVPs。结果是一个随机少步生成器，其 FID 在采样预算从 4 增长到 50 NFE 时不会退化，且相同方法可迁移到文本到图像生成。代码和预训练模型见 https://github.com/CompVis/iDDM。

## Abstract
Distributional Diffusion Models (DDMs) replace the standard mean-prediction denoiser with a \emph{distributional} denoiser trained via a scoring rule objective, learning a stochastic approximation to $p(x_1 \mid x_t)$ rather than its conditional mean. However, scaling DDMs to modern image-generation settings faces two obstacles: (i) multi-particle training incurs overhead that scales with the number of particles, (ii) DDMs use globally fixed scoring rule hyperparameters, forcing a single trade-off across sampling budgets. We mitigate these limitations by deferring particle expansion to late transformer layers, and the hyperparameter trade-off by introducing time-dependent scoring rule schedules informed by the dynamical regimes of~\citet{Biroli2024}. Combined with a DiT-based latent setup, these changes make DDM training practical on class-conditional ImageNet-$256^2$, achieving 4.48 FID at 4 steps and 2.38 at 50 steps with DiT-XL/2, from a single model trained from scratch in one stage, without a teacher, self-distillation or JVPs. The result is a stochastic few-step generator whose FID does not degrade as the sampling budget grows from 4 to 50 NFE, and the same recipe transfers to text-to-image generation. Code and pre-trained models available at https://github.com/CompVis/iDDM.
