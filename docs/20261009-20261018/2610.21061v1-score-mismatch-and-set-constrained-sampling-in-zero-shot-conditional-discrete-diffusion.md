## 摘要
离散扩散模型已成为在多种应用中建模复杂离散分布的有力框架。尽管理论收敛保证依赖于目标分布与训练分布之间的一致性，但这种情况在实践中很少成立，使得分数不匹配成为离散扩散采样中的核心挑战。这与零样本条件生成尤为相关：在该任务中，在无条件分布上训练的模型在推理时被直接复用来从条件目标分布中采样。本文首次对分数不匹配的离散扩散模型进行理论分析，并推导出一个通用的 KL 散度上界，清晰刻画了此类不匹配所导致的额外偏差。我们的分析涵盖部分观测约束，包括文本填充、句子补全以及更一般的逐坐标集合约束。具体而言，我们分析了经典的 Conditional SEDD 采样器，并表明它可能产生一个随维度、词表大小和兼容性缩放的偏差项。基于该分析，我们提出一种用于逐坐标集合约束的随机硬钳制（Randomized Hard Clamping, RHC）采样器。我们证明，分配概率可以由带有吸收率的无条件分数精确表示。最后，数值实验和真实数据实验验证了所提出的 RHC 采样器的有效性。

## Abstract
Discrete diffusion models have become a powerful framework for modeling complex discrete distributions across a range of applications. While theoretical convergence guarantees depend on consistency between the target and training distributions, such a case rarely holds in practice, making score mismatch a central challenge in discrete diffusion sampling. This is particularly relevant to zero-shot conditional generation, where a model trained on the unconditional distribution is directly reused at inference time to sample from a conditional target distribution. In this paper, we provide the first theoretical analysis for score-mismatched discrete diffusion models and derive a general KL divergence upper bound that clearly identifies the additional bias caused by such a mismatch. Our analysis covers partial-observation constraints, including text infilling, sentence completion, and more general coordinatewise set constraints. Specifically, we analyze the classical Conditional SEDD sampler and show that it can incur a bias term that scales with the dimension, vocabulary size, and compatibility. Based on this analysis, we propose a Randomized Hard Clamping (RHC) sampler for coordinatewise set constraints. We show that the assignment probabilities can be expressed exactly by the unconditional scores with absorbing rate. Finally, numerical and real-data experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed RHC sampler.
